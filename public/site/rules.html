AlgoNex 1.0 · Rulebook

Rules & Regulations

Please read every rule before registering. Taking part means you agree to all of them.

AlgoNex is an open-source hackathon. Every project must be published publicly under an approved open-source licence.

1. Eligibility

  1. Open to all college students with a valid student ID card.
  2. Students from any branch, year or university may take part.
  3. Each participant can be in only one team.

2. Teams

  1. Teams must have 2 to 6 members.
  2. Teams from different colleges are allowed.
  3. At least one woman member is encouraged.
  4. Team members cannot change after registration closes.
  5. Registration fee is ₹500 per member and is not refundable.

3. Open-source rules

  1. All code must be in a public GitHub repository from the start of the hackathon.
  2. Use an OSI-approved licence such as MIT, Apache 2.0 or GPL-3.0. Add a LICENSE file to the repository.
  3. Open-source libraries, frameworks and APIs are allowed. Credit them in your README.
  4. Commits must be made during the hackathon. Commit regularly so judges can see your progress.
  5. Your README must explain the problem, how to install and run the project, and a short demo.
  6. Teams keep ownership of their work. Because projects are open source, anyone may reuse them under the licence.

4. Building your project

  1. Pick one problem statement from the Tracks page, or choose Open Innovation.
  2. All building must happen within the 24-hour hackathon window.
  3. You may plan ideas and designs beforehand, but not write code beforehand.
  4. AI tools are allowed. Tell the judges how you used them.
  5. Copying another team's work or past projects means instant disqualification.

5. Judging & submission

  1. Submit your repository link, a demo video (up to 3 min) and a presentation before the deadline.
  2. Projects are judged on innovation, technical depth, impact, open-source quality and presentation.
  3. The judges' decision is final.

6. Code of conduct

  • Be respectful to all participants, mentors, volunteers and staff.
  • Harassment or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated.
  • Look after the venue and university property.
  • No alcohol, smoking or illegal substances on campus.
  • Organisers may disqualify any team that breaks these rules.

Organisers may update these rules. Any changes will be shared with all registered teams.
